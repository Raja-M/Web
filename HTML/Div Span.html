Marri Laxman Reddy Institute of Technology and Management

Marri Laxman Reddy Institute of Technology and Management (MLRITM), Hyderabad, stands tall as one of the most reputed autonomous engineering institutions in Telangana, driven by a commitment to innovation, excellence, and holistic student development. Established in 2009, the institute is approved by AICTE, recognized by the Government of Telangana, and permanently affiliated to JNTU Hyderabad, with NAAC ‘A’ Grade and NBA accreditation for all eligible programs. Recognized as a Scientific and Industrial Research Organization (SIRO) by the DSIR, MLRITM integrates strong academics, research, and entrepreneurship to nurture globally competent engineers and leaders.
