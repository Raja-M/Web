  • The New Bishops from various parts of the world 

    The New Bishops course is an annual even held early in each year in Canterbury Cathedral and brings together a number of the Communion’s bishops for an opportunity to learn from each other, to learn from Anglican Communion staff and to build networks and friendships across cultural and geographical divides. The new bishops taking part in the course this year are from the Provinces of Australia, Bermuda, Canada, Central Africa, England, Japan, Kenya, Korea, Melanesia, South India (Karimnagar Diocese), Pakistan, Papua New Guinea, Sudan and South Sudan, Tanzania, and West Africa.Read More →
