My Cities

London

London is the capital of England. London is the capital and largest city of the United Kingdom, boasting a population of over 9 million, a 2,000-year history founded by Romans, and a reputation as a global hub for arts, finance, and culture. Situated on the River Thames, it is known for iconic landmarks like Big Ben, the Tower of London, and the London Eye.

Paris

Paris, the capital of France and the "City of Light," is a global hub for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture with a population of over 2 million (13 million+ metro). Situated on the Seine River, it is famous for landmarks like the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral.

Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital and largest city of Japan, serving as a global hub for culture, finance, and technology with a population exceeding 14 million (41 million in the greater area). It merges modern, high-tech life with traditional, historical elements, offering Michelin-starred dining, bustling, orderly streets, and iconic, ancient temples like Meiji Jingu.
