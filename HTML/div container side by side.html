Hidtory

Born from a vision to redefine the landscape of technical education, Marri Laxman Reddy Institute of Technology and Management (MLRITM), Hyderabad, was established in 2009 under the aegis of the Marri Educational Society, a name synonymous with commitment to academic excellence and nation-building through education.
